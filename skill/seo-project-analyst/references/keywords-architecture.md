# Keyword research and architecture

Start with business goals, audience, context of use and need. Build seeds from products/services and problems they solve; expand using current SERPs, customer questions, competitors and available tools. Label brainstormed terms separately from measured keywords. Never invent volume or difficulty.

Preserve original Persian spelling and a separate normalized matching field if needed. Normalize Arabic/Persian characters, digits and whitespace deliberately; avoid merging different product models, capacities, brands or locations. Preserve exact URLs and exact exported queries in evidence.

Cluster by actual need and SERP/page compatibility, not identical broad intent labels. Two commercial queries can need distinct pages. Review ambiguous clusters manually, then assign a preferred existing URL or proposed page type with rationale. Do not automatically target the homepage or create one page per keyword.

Audit existing information architecture first: categories, product/service pages, articles, hubs, breadcrumbs and navigation. Look for orphan pages using an inventory independent of the crawl, excessive depth relative to importance, duplicated categories and broken internal links. Label an apparent orphan as a candidate if no independent inventory exists.

Prioritize commercial relevance, achievable differentiation, demand evidence and effort. A low difficulty score or missing exact-match title is only a screening signal. Identify a specific value gap the site can fill.

Deliver keyword/cluster, audience need, evidence, current/proposed URL, page type, business value, ambiguity and next action. Keep every volume associated with its own keyword and original source/date when sorting.
