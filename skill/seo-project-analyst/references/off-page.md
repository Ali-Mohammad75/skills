# Off-page analysis

Inventory available referring domains, target URLs, anchors, link attributes, acquisition dates and evidence of relevance. Verify samples when live access exists. Different tools have different indexes; DR/DA, raw backlink count and TLD are not Google scores or standalone measures of value.

Review actual editorial context and business/audience relevance. Set acquisition mix and timing per project, goals, competition, budget and target pages; course percentages and schedules are examples. If considering paid or tier-link tactics from notes, distinguish the owner-selected tactic from official policy and measured evidence. Explain relevant paid-link policy risks without silently replacing the owner’s strategy with a universal ban or pretending payment is organic endorsement. Do not claim that making a link look natural makes it compliant. Distinguish advertising/sponsorship with appropriate attributes from earned editorial links; verify current spam policies.

Recommend specific link-worthy assets: original research, useful tools, expert contributions, documented projects and genuinely helpful resources. For outreach draft relevant pitches and explain why the recipient's audience benefits. Do not send messages without explicit authorization and recipient verification in the host environment.

Treat toxic-link scores as investigation cues, not automatic disavow/delete instructions. Review current official disavow guidance and actual evidence before proposing it. Deliver observed patterns, limitations, opportunities, proposed targets and effort; never guarantee rankings or prescribe an acquisition calendar independent of the project.
