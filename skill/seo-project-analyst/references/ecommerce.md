# Ecommerce SEO

Identify business-critical categories/products using actual margin, stock and conversion data where available. Do not invent an 80/20 distribution. Map generic category intent and specific model intent to appropriate pages rather than always favoring categories or products.

Inspect filtered URLs, sorting, internal search, pagination and variants. Determine which filter combinations offer durable search value, distinct inventory and useful content. Avoid indexing every combination or blocking all parameters blindly. Existing indexed URLs may need an accessible noindex/removal strategy before long-term crawl controls. Canonicals must reflect actual duplication.

Check product identifiers, variant relationships, selected variation URLs, visible price/currency/availability and structured data consistency. Validate exact model/capacity/brand against manufacturer or approved supplier data. Distinguish feed eligibility and connector/country availability from ordinary organic SEO; verify Merchant Center support before promising it.

For temporarily out-of-stock products, assess continued information value and restock expectations. For permanently discontinued items, consider useful retained documentation, genuinely equivalent replacements, or removal; do not automatically redirect to a broad category. Review internal links and sitemaps with each choice.

Evaluate category descriptions and guides for buyer usefulness without burying product listings. Check transactional friction using actual device/checkout behavior and measurement. Draft corrective actions with SKU/URL scope, evidence and validation; avoid changing price, stock or checkout settings from an SEO audit alone.
