# Competitor and AI visibility gaps

Choose competitors by audience, market and SERP overlap rather than brand size alone. With keyword data, separate missing, weak and already-covered topics while retaining date, country and provider estimates. With backlink data, compare referring domains against the same provider/time scope and verify relevant candidate links; missing from the provider index is not proof of absence.

For comparison pages verify product facts, pricing dates, limitations and the user's own competitive claims. Give a balanced decision framework and record unknown values. Do not invent competitor weaknesses to create a conversion angle.

For AI visibility specify engine, language, geography, date, exact prompt set, sample size and repeated-run policy. Separate linked citations, unlinked brand mentions and position within an answer. Define numerator and denominator of share of voice before calculating; do not compare percentages built from different prompt universes. Keep prompt-level evidence and label a sample as a sample.

Map each gap to a specific page or asset, business need, evidence and next action. A provider integration unlocks data access; it does not prove the recommendations cause higher rankings or citations.
