# Specialized search surfaces

## Discover and news
Separate interest-driven content planning from query-driven SEO while allowing overlap. Define a real audience, context, topics, timely angle and useful value. The note’s entity examples are educational, not a compulsory named-entity requirement for topic selection. Plan distribution to that audience without inventing traffic or using fake engagement.

Verify Discover policies, image requirements and preview settings in current documentation. Apply the owner’s exact 1200 px image-width rule. Do not impose a six-month domain minimum or compulsory special schema. Preserve the owner’s heuristic that content usually has a 72-hour life and regular publication sustains inflow; this is not a hard expiry, entry deadline, prescribed assessment window or traffic guarantee. Avoid misleading, outrage-only or withheld-information headlines. Distinguish Discover, News and Top Stories eligibility/reporting. Check visible dates, accurate update dates, authorship, publication identity and news sitemap needs where appropriate.

## Health and other YMYL
Treat author/reviewer identity, editorial process, evidence and update practices as real operations, not decorative trust badges. Require appropriate primary evidence for consequential claims. Mark facts needing qualified review. Schema markup and a policy page cannot establish expertise that does not exist; avoid unsupported guaranteed outcomes.

## AI search
Identify the platform before advising on crawling, discovery, citations or measurement. Verify current official guidance; AI platforms have different crawlers and controls. For Google's AI search features, retain foundational indexability and useful content requirements instead of promising a special schema or llms.txt requirement.

Develop attributable original evidence, clear entity identity and useful answers. Measure observable referrals, conversions and sampled citations with explicit sampling limits. A mention in a single prompt is not market-wide visibility; zero referral data is not proof of no exposure. Separate published platform requirements from GEO/AEO experiments.
