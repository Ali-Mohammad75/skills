# Evidence and owner choices

Read [owner decisions](owner-decisions.md) first. This reference distinguishes chosen methods from official requirements; it does not override the owner or authorize new methodology. All additions recorded there were approved, while future conflicts require consultation.

- Indexing API: the owner chose selective use for important URLs, beyond documented Google support where applicable. Preserve this preference while identifying scope and unknown outcomes; never forge eligible schema or bypass limits.
- Discover: no six-month gate. Exact 1200 px width is the owner house specification. The 72-hour note is a usual-lifespan/regular-publication heuristic, not expiry or entry timing. Entity examples are educational.
- CTR and pruning: low CTR deserves explicit investigation; it is not alone a removal criterion. Multiple URLs/query need intent and performance evidence before merging. Broad intent labels do not decide page grouping.
- Updates: correct/replace obsolete text while retaining useful coverage; strikethrough only where useful to a reader, not as an established ranking protection technique. Record baseline and monitor.
- Outbound anchors and competitor restrictions: apply owner-specific source-name/Naked-URL rules; do not mislabel these as official Google requirements.
- Local SEO: owner priority is Maps, then Neshan/Balad for Iran; verify eligibility elsewhere. Geotags/CID are chosen local tasks. VPS requirement is scoped to operators in Iran working on foreign local sites.
- Comments and ratings: follow the editorial/manual workflow in [content enrichment](content-enrichment.md). Preserve synthetic/manual origin in work records and never invent evidence that it came from users. Explain actual structured-data eligibility rather than promising stars.
- Robots.txt, noindex and canonical: keep crawling/indexing controls distinct and verify actual implementation and evidence.
- Paid links and tool metrics: owner strategy does not make a tactic an official recommendation or a proprietary score a Google metric. Provide source/date when discussing current policies.

## Content-update research accepted by owner

Reviewed 2026-09-11. [Ahrefs republishing workflow](https://ahrefs.com/blog/republishing-content/) describes limited updates and rewrites, with case observations rather than controlled guarantees. [Yoast maintenance guidance](https://yoast.com/keep-your-content-fresh-and-up-to-date/) describes replacing an old version with an updated draft. [Google core-update guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates) supports useful rewriting/restructuring and warns against arbitrary quick fixes. No substantiated evidence was found in the reviewed sources that strikethrough prevents ranking drops or deleting an obsolete paragraph necessarily triggers a prolonged demotion. Do not generalize that absence of evidence into a claim that edits can never reduce performance.

For changed platform facts, verify current primary documentation when possible; otherwise state the date and offline limitation. Ask the owner only about a concrete unresolved methodological conflict, not every routine implementation choice.
