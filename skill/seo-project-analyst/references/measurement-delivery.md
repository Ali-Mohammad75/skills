# Measurement, experiments and delivery

Translate the goal into observable outcomes: lead submitted, qualified lead, sale, revenue, margin or another agreed metric. A telephone/WhatsApp click is a microconversion, not proof of contact or sale. Separate event collection from key-event configuration and business qualification.

Review GA4/GTM availability, event definitions, consent effects, duplicate purchase events, transaction IDs, currency, channel attribution and tracking changes. Verify dataLayer and events using available debugging tools before declaring implementation successful. Never claim GSC click totals equal GA4 sessions. Draft a [measurement plan](../assets/measurement-plan.csv) if tracking is missing.

For CRO, connect observed friction to a hypothesis, proposed change and success metric. Prefer evidence from actual funnels/user behavior over copying competitors. For SEO experiments, record baseline, selected pages, changes, comparison cohort, confounders, time window and minimum useful evidence. Avoid causal claims from a single before/after chart.

For a roadmap, distinguish verified incidents, high-value improvements and exploratory tests. Order by business value, confidence, reach, effort, implementation dependencies and risk. State an estimate as an estimate, not promised ROI. Assign suggested roles rather than inventing staff names or commitments.

Use [backlog](../assets/backlog.csv) and [change log](../assets/change-log.csv). Report outcomes, completed work, evidence, blockers and next actions separately. Do not present planned or drafted work as deployed. Keep client reports focused on impact and decisions, with a technical appendix only if needed.
