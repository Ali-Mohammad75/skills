# Local and international SEO

## Local services
Confirm real business identity, service area, customer eligibility and conversion route. Build location pages only where they provide distinct useful service evidence. Avoid near-identical city pages that funnel users without added value. Use real availability, contact details, projects and photos; never fabricate addresses or reviews.

Verify Google Business Profile eligibility and country support for the current business before recommending creation or management. Website SEO, maps visibility and business-profile features are distinct. For Iranian businesses prioritize Google Maps presence and accurate information, then Neshan and Balad. Include geotag and verified Google Maps CID tasks for every local project as owner methods. Require VPS specifically for operators working from Iran on foreign local projects; do not extend this to all local work or assume the VPS location. Do not bypass provider restrictions. These preferences are not guaranteed ranking mechanisms. Use local SERP evidence with stated location/device and compare appropriate competitors.

## Languages and countries
Identify language versus country targets, actual translations, currencies and fulfillment. Assess URL structure choices against maintenance and business needs. Check hreflang reciprocals, valid codes, self references, equivalent page mappings and relevant x-default where used. Check canonical consistency; do not canonicalize every language to the source page when each should be indexed.

Do not equate an English site with one target country or claim server country alone determines rankings. Inspect forced IP/language redirects and crawler access. Validate localized intent and product availability, not just translation. Deliver language/region/page mappings and evidence-based corrections.
